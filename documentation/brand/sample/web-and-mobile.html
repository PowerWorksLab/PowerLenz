See where your electricity goes.

PowerLenz measures your home's supply at one point and aims to show which appliances are using it. Designed for 230 V, single-phase homes in India.

From one clamp to a clear picture

1

Sense the supply

A current transformer and a voltage sensor read the incoming line.

2

Sample and compute

An ADS131M04 converter and an ESP32-S3 turn the signals into power readings.

3

Show the usage

Readings go to the cloud, where appliance patterns are identified and displayed.
