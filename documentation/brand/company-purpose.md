# Sixteen Amps: Company Purpose

Status: working version, 28 November 2026. The two lines below are **final**. The rest is the current draft and will change as we learn from users.

## Lines

| | Line | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Tagline** | **Clarity for every unit of energy.** | With the logo |
| **Story line** | **From every socket to every decision.** | Posters and presentations |

"Unit" also means the kilowatt-hour on an Indian electricity bill. The socket refers to the 16 A socket behind the company name.

## Why we exist

Energy decisions are often made without seeing clearly where electricity goes, whether in a home, across a utility's network or in public policy.

Sixteen Amps is about giving **users** clarity. Users can be households, utility partners, government and others. Households are where we start, not the limit of the business.

## Mission

Turn electricity data into clear, trustworthy information that people can act on.

## Vision

Everyone who uses, supplies or governs energy can see it clearly enough to make better decisions.

## Users

| User | Question they want answered | What we could offer | What it demands from us |
|---|---|---|---|
| Households | What is driving my bill? | Live use, appliance breakdown, alerts | Simplicity and affordability |
| Utility partners | How and when is demand building up? | Grouped load patterns, peak insights, saving programmes | Accuracy, scale and dependable integration |
| Government and policymakers | Where would an efficiency measure matter most? | Anonymised regional trends | Representative data, privacy and compliance |
| Businesses and institutions (suggested) | Which loads are wasting energy? | Site-level monitoring | Likely three-phase hardware |
| Researchers (suggested) | How is energy really used? | Consented, anonymised datasets | Clear consent and data quality |

## Values

1. **Clarity:** information that leads to a decision, not just more numbers.
2. **Trust:** say what is measured, what is estimated and how confident we are.
3. **Privacy and consent:** people control their household data; partners see grouped data only with permission.
4. **Safety:** no compromise on mains-connected hardware.

## Product

**PowerLenz by Sixteen Amps** is the first product: an energy monitor for Indian single-phase homes (230 V, 50 Hz). Design details are in [`../README.md`](../README.md).

## Roadmap

| Stage | Focus | Proof needed |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Prove | PowerLenz measures a single-phase supply | Agreement with a reference meter |
| 2. Understand | It identifies major appliances in homes | Tested identification accuracy |
| 3. Partner | Pilot with a utility or institution | One partner finds the insight useful |
| 4. Inform | Regional insight for public bodies | Enough consenting, representative users |

## What we may and may not claim

- Nothing has been built or bench-tested yet. Present appliance identification and savings as aims.
- Do not describe PowerLenz as a billing meter or a certified meter.
- The needs of utilities and government are assumptions until we speak to them.
